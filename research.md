My research examines the **cybersecurity, safety, reliability and assurance of autonomous systems**.

### Research themes

- **Cybersecurity of autonomous systems:** adversarial sensor manipulation, GNSS spoofing, system-level consequences, and red teaming.
- **Trustworthy learning-enabled autonomy:** robust reinforcement learning, adaptive decision-making, distribution shift, and runtime assurance.
- **AI/ML assurance:** connecting empirical robustness evidence with safety and security arguments.
- **Cyber-physical systems:** state estimation, networked control, resilience, digital twins, and smart energy systems.
