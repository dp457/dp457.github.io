<section class="research-page-intro">
  <p class="section-eyebrow">RESEARCH OVERVIEW</p>
  <h1>Research</h1>
  <p class="section-lead">My research examines the <strong>cybersecurity, safety, reliability and assurance of autonomous systems</strong>, with particular attention to how learning-enabled and cyber-physical systems behave under uncertainty and adversarial conditions.</p>
</section>
<section class="research-page-section" aria-labelledby="research-themes">
  <h2 id="research-themes">Research themes</h2>
  <div class="research-theme-grid">
    <article class="research-theme-card">
      <span class="theme-index">01 / SECURITY</span>
      <h3>Cybersecurity of autonomous systems</h3>
      <p>Adversarial sensor manipulation, GNSS spoofing, and the consequences of cyber attacks on integrated autonomous-system behaviour.</p>
      <p class="theme-keywords">Attack modelling · System-level consequences · Red teaming</p>
    </article>
    <article class="research-theme-card">
      <span class="theme-index">02 / AUTONOMY</span>
      <h3>Trustworthy learning-enabled autonomy</h3>
      <p>Robust reinforcement learning, adaptive decision-making and resilience to distribution shifts in autonomous systems.</p>
      <p class="theme-keywords">Robust RL · Distribution shift · Runtime assurance</p>
    </article>
    <article class="research-theme-card">
      <span class="theme-index">03 / ASSURANCE</span>
      <h3>AI/ML assurance</h3>
      <p>Connecting empirical evidence of robustness and reliability with structured safety and security arguments for learning-enabled systems.</p>
      <p class="theme-keywords">Robustness evidence · Safety arguments · Security assurance</p>
    </article>
    <article class="research-theme-card">
      <span class="theme-index">04 / CYBER-PHYSICAL SYSTEMS</span>
      <h3>Cyber-physical systems</h3>
      <p>State estimation, networked control and resilience, alongside data-driven modelling and digital twins for smart energy systems.</p>
      <p class="theme-keywords">State estimation · Networked control · Digital twins</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
